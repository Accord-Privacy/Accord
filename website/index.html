Built for Privacy

Every feature designed from the ground up to protect your conversations.

🔐

End-to-End Encryption

Double Ratchet protocol for DMs. Encrypted channels for group conversations. Nobody but the participants can read your messages — not even us.

Voice Channels

Real-time voice, relay-routed by default. Voice is not end-to-end encrypted yet: the relay operator can listen to relay-mode calls, and P2P mode and screen sharing reveal your IP address to other participants.

How It Works

A simple architecture with zero trust assumptions.

1

Relays Are Dumb Pipes

Relays route encrypted blobs between clients and are designed so they can't read your messages. Today a relay can still read relay-mode voice and files uploaded before encrypted attachments shipped, it sees attachment metadata such as padded sizes, and a malicious relay can intercept encrypted sessions when they are set up.

2

Nodes Are Communities

Each community runs as a Node with its own channels, roles, and settings. Nodes connect through relays but own their own data.

3

Messages Are E2EE

Messages and attachments are encrypted on your device and decrypted on the recipient's; every file gets its own random key, sent only inside the encrypted message. Your private keys never leave your machine. Voice is not end-to-end encrypted yet.
