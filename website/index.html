Built for Privacy

Every feature designed from the ground up to protect your conversations.

🔐

End-to-End Encryption

Double Ratchet protocol for DMs. Encrypted channels for group conversations. Nobody but the participants can read your messages — not even us.

🎙️

Voice Channels

Real-time voice and screen sharing through the relay, end-to-end encrypted with a new key for every change of participants and every 10 minutes, sent only inside your encrypted sessions with the other participants. The peer-to-peer mode of older versions is removed, so other participants never learn your IP address from a call. Not yet verified: until identity verification ships, the relay decides who counts as a member. Screen sharing is video only, reaches only the participants who choose Watch, and needs a relay that supports encrypted screen sharing; the relay still sees who shares and watches and the size and timing of the video. The macOS app can watch screen shares but not share. Voice does not work in the iOS and Android apps yet.

🏠

Community Nodes

Like Discord servers, but privacy-first. Create and manage communities with channels, roles, and permissions — all under your control.

🤖

Airgapped Bot API

Bots interact through a sandboxed API and never see message content. Extend functionality without compromising privacy.

🖥️

Self-Hostable

Run your own relay on a $5 VPS or a spare desktop. Full sovereignty over your infrastructure. No vendor lock-in, ever.

📖

Open Source

Licensed under AGPL-3.0. Every line of code is auditable. Trust is verified, not assumed. The Rust voice modules in the repository (srtp, voice, p2p_voice and jitter_buffer) are unused: no shipped app uses them.

How It Works

A simple architecture with zero trust assumptions.

1

Relays Are Dumb Pipes

Relays route encrypted blobs between clients and are designed so they can't read your messages. Today a relay can still read files uploaded before encrypted attachments shipped and the voice of outdated apps, it sees attachment and call metadata such as padded sizes and timing, and a malicious relay can intercept encrypted sessions when they are set up.

2

Nodes Are Communities

Each community runs as a Node with its own channels, roles, and settings. Nodes connect through relays but own their own data.

3

Messages Are E2EE

Messages and attachments are encrypted on your device and decrypted on the recipient's; every file gets its own random key, sent only inside the encrypted message. Your private keys never leave your machine. Calls and screen sharing are end-to-end encrypted too, but not yet verified.

Security Model

Understand exactly who can see what. No fine print.

👤

Your Client

Holds your keys. Encrypts and decrypts all content locally.
